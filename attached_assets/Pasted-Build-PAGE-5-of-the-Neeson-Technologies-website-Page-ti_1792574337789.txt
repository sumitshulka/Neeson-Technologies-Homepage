1. PURPOSE OF THE PAGE
==================================================

The page has one primary objective:

Convert a visitor with a genuine technical/business question into a
30-minute discovery call or enquiry.

The visitor should be able to:

1. Book a 30-minute discovery call
2. Choose a convenient time
3. Submit a short enquiry
4. Email Neeson
5. Connect via WhatsApp
6. Visit LinkedIn
7. Request a callback if required

The page should NOT feel like a generic "Contact Us" page.

It should feel like:

"Let's establish whether Neeson is the right fit."

The central message:

"Bring one question.
Leave with a next step."

==================================================
